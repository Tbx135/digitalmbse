Additive Manufacturing

In-house additive capability for fixtures, jigs, brackets, and rapid iteration that supports forward deployed engineering and RaaS work.

Overview

Digital MBSE Solutions LLC operates in-house additive manufacturing on a production FDM printer farm to speed robotics integration work. The printers exist to cut turnaround on the physical bits that make cell bring-up faster: fixtures, jigs, brackets, sensor mounts, and iteration prototypes that support forward deployed engineering and RaaS engagements.

FDM printer farm

Single printer, multi-printer farm layout, and printed fixture examples.

What we use it for

Practical parts that unblock robotics integration.

Fixtures & jigs

Hold-downs, alignment aids, and workholding for cell bring-up, tooling trials, and repeatable demos in non-irradiated environments.

Brackets & mounts

Sensor, camera, and peripheral mounts when commercial hardware doesn’t match cell geometry on day one.

Rapid iteration

Same-day or next-day design–print–fit cycles so forward deployed work doesn’t stall waiting on machine shops for every plastic part.

Support for RaaS / FDE

Physical enablement for sensing, twin, and teleop/autonomy workflows — complementary to systems engineering, not a substitute for it.
