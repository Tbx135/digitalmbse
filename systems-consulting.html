Systems Engineering Consulting

MBSE, architecture, HIL/SIL, V&V, and safety-critical systems engineering — delivered as forward deployed engineering services.

Consulting aligned with forward deployed work

Systems engineering consulting from Digital MBSE Solutions LLC is not a standalone slide-only practice. It is grounded in the same forward deployed engineering model used for research labs and high-hazard cells: architecture and MBSE that connect to real integration, verification that operators can inspect, and safety-critical habits from medical device and nuclear backgrounds.

Engagements may stand alone (architecture / V&V / MBSE coaching) or blend with RaaS and additive support when physical cell work is in scope.

Service areas

Grounded in the team’s experience across surgical robotics, medical devices, and nuclear engineering.

Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE)

Requirements, architecture, and hazard-aware modeling practices; SE/MBSE training roadmaps; INCOSE-aligned practices.

Architecture & CMU SEI practices

Software and system architecture using Carnegie Mellon SEI principles — replatforming, green-field platforms, and multi-team delivery structure.

HIL / SIL & verification environments

Automated HIL/SIL test environments, motion-capture spaces, and C++/Python regression suites drawn from surgical robotics verification experience.

V&V and promotion discipline

Test strategy, configuration control, and evidence-before-promotion habits suited to teleop/autonomy and regulated software contexts.

Safety-critical SE — medical device

Team experience in surgical and orthopedic robotics and medical device software — architecture, verification, and regulated delivery.

Safety-critical SE — nuclear

Team experience in nuclear engineering, safety-critical simulation software, and probabilistic risk assessment (PRA) contexts.
