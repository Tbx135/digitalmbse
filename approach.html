Approach

Forward deployed engineering services paired with RaaS — embed where the cell is, then productize sensing, twin, and gated AI.

Forward deployed engineering services

We place experienced systems, software, V&V, and MBSE talent close to your facility and cell — working alongside your operators, safety stakeholders, and existing contractors. The goal is rapid, honest progress on integration, not a remote slide deck.

  • On-site / embedded engagement for cell integration, SW/HW bring-up, and procedure-aligned workflows
  • Architecture and MBSE that connect requirements, hazards, and verification early
  • HIL/SIL-minded test strategy so teleop and autonomy earn promotion through evidence
  • Vendor and tooling coordination when portfolios span multiple suppliers

Robot-as-a-Service (RaaS)

RaaS is how we package the repeatable stack for DOE user facilities and high-hazard cells: edge/high-speed sensing, a live digital twin, and AI-in-the-loop training with test-driven promotion gates — spanning teleoperation and autonomous modes.

  • Phase I: on-site KUKA remote-handling–class cell for non-irradiated demos and workflow development
  • Phase II: humanoid garage POC — early proof-of-concept only; not production or facility qualification

How the two fit together

Forward deployed engineering gets the right people into the cell and the right decisions made. RaaS provides the sensing / twin / gated-AI spine so each engagement leaves reusable capability rather than only one-off scripts. In-house additive manufacturing accelerates fixtures and jigs; systems consulting deepens MBSE and V&V. Design partners help shape all of it.
