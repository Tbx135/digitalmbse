About

Digital MBSE Solutions LLC — Pennsylvania. Forward deployed engineering services and RaaS for research labs and high-hazard cells.

Company

Digital MBSE Solutions LLC provides forward deployed engineering services and robot-as-a-service (RaaS) for research labs, laboratory facilities, and high-hazard cells (hot cells, remote handling, clean rooms), with adjacent interest in next-generation nuclear plant remote maintenance. Supporting lines include in-house additive manufacturing and systems engineering consulting. We are actively seeking design partners.

The team

20+ years of experience across nuclear power, orthopedic and surgical robotics, and medical devices.

Experience

Our team has led multi-disciplinary engineering organizations in mission-critical, safety-critical domains — building robotic systems, software platforms, and verification programs where evidence matters.

  • Robotics — robotic platform development, real-time controls, embedded firmware, and integration
  • Surgical robotics and medtech software — architecture, delivery, and regulated release
  • Nuclear engineering — fuel design and simulation software, safety-critical simulation, and risk analysis
  • Systems engineering and MBSE — requirements, architecture, and model-based practices
  • Software verification — automated HIL/SIL environments, regression suites, and V&V organizations
  • Regulated product development — medical device and nuclear quality expectations
  • Green-field engineering — standing up new teams, platforms, and cloud data and simulation systems

Education

Affiliations

IEEE, INCOSE, and SAFe.

Standards background

Professional familiarity from prior roles with IEC 62304, ISO 13485, NQA-1, 10 CFR 50, and FDA/MDR contexts.
