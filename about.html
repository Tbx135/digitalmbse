About

Digital MBSE Solutions LLC — Hummelstown, PA. Forward deployed engineering services and RaaS for high-hazard cells.

Company

Digital MBSE Solutions LLC (brand: digitalmbse.com) provides forward deployed engineering services and robot-as-a-service (RaaS) for DOE user facilities and high-hazard cells (hot cells, remote handling, clean rooms), with adjacent interest in next-generation nuclear plant remote maintenance. Supporting lines include in-house additive manufacturing and systems engineering consulting. We are actively seeking design partners. This site does not invent customers, partnerships, or LOIs.

Team depth: approximately 40 years combined experience in safety-critical robotics and software. Principal Investigator / founder Daniel S. Menoher.
