Product stack: RaaS spine

Three layers that make remote-handling robotics observable, trainable, and promotable.

1

Edge / high-speed sensing

Capture cell and robot state at rates useful for remote handling, operator awareness, and closed-loop review — the foundation for twin and AI training data.

2

Live digital twin

A living operational picture for situational awareness, rehearsal, and post-run analysis — not a static CAD dump.

3

AI-in-the-loop training with test-driven promotion gates

Human-in-the-loop teleop and autonomous modes. Behaviors advance only when they pass agreed tests — suitable for high-hazard cells where demos are not enough.
